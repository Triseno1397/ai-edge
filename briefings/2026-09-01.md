**The EU designates ChatGPT its first-ever AI "Very Large Online Platform" under the DSA** — The European Commission ruled ChatGPT crossed the 45M-monthly-EU-user threshold (it hit ~159M), putting it under the bloc's strictest online-safety regime alongside Reddit and Roblox. OpenAI has until end of December 2026 to comply.

Why it's big: This is the first time an AI chatbot — not a search engine or social platform — has been pulled under the DSA's strictest tier, setting the legal template regulators worldwide will use to classify AI assistants and forcing systemic risk audits and algorithmic transparency no other AI lab currently faces.

### Viral Prompts

**Hero product orbit (turntable spin)** — Written for Seedance 2; motion-only prompting so the reference image, not the prompt, carries product/label fidelity.

```
Slow 360-degree orbit around the referenced product, 8 seconds, even rotation, soft front-key plus rim light, shallow depth of field, label remains crisp and centered, subtle floor reflection, locked focal length, no zoom, no cuts.
```

`VIDEO` `ADS`

Key rule from the source: never re-describe the bottle/label/color in the prompt itself — that's the #1 cause of label drift and palette shift across generations. Let the reference image own identity; let the prompt own camera/light/duration only.

→ For you: Use as the base template for every product-orbit deliverable, then swap only lighting/reflection cues per brand mood — keeps output consistent across a whole catalog batch.
