# Entertainment Disruption Map

A living map of where AI is moving across the **entire** live-production / broadcast stack — not just
camera/video — and where Big Boy's opening is to **own** that change rather than be displaced by it.
The `ai-intel-briefing` skill updates this whenever `/disrupt`, `/entertainment`, or a full brief turns
up something new. This is the strategic spine of the whole project: the goal isn't to watch the
industry change, it's to be the one changing it.

**Legend — Maturity:** `NOW` (deployable today) · `NEAR` (6–18 mo) · `HORIZON` (further out).

---

## Video / Camera (his home department)
- **Auto-framing / AI tracking on robotic & PTZ cams** — `NOW`. Threat to operator headcount on
  multi-cam shoots. → His angle: deploy/integrate AI-tracked robo systems; sell the configured rig +
  operating layer, not just the camera.
- **Gen-video at broadcast quality (Kling/Luma/Veo/Runway)** — `NOW`. Threat to B-roll, inserts, plates,
  some VFX. → His angle: a managed gen-video shot service for productions and brands; multi-model routing.
- **Live vertical/clip generation (e.g. AWS Elemental Inference)** — `NOW`. Threat to clip editors. →
  His angle: an AI live-clipping/social-cutdown service sold to events, sports, broadcasters.

## Switching / Replay / Graphics
- **AI auto-switching & auto-replay / highlight detection** — `NEAR`. Threat to TD and replay/EVS roles
  on lower-tier productions. → His angle: be the integrator who deploys it; own the config + fallback.
- **Generative graphics, lower-thirds, real-time captioning/translation** — `NOW`. Threat to graphics
  ops and caption vendors. → His angle: managed AI graphics/localization layer for live shows.

## Audio
- **AI mixing, dialogue isolation/cleanup, RF de-noise** — `NEAR`. → His angle: AI audio-cleanup service
  for post and live record.
- **Voice cloning & dubbing/localization** — `NOW`. Threat to VO and dub houses. → His angle: dubbing/
  localization pipeline for content owners.

## Lighting / Rigging / Staging / Scenic
- **AI previz, lighting design assist, automation programming** — `NEAR`/`HORIZON`. → His angle: AI
  previz-to-program tooling; less direct for him, watch and partner.

## Media Servers / Content Prep
- **Automated content prep, versioning, conform** — `NEAR`. → His angle: internal automation he can
  productize for content/playback teams.

## IT / Networking / IP Video / Transmission
- **Cloud production (ST 2110 in cloud), AI encoding/QC, automated transport** — `NOW`/`NEAR`. Plays to
  his engineering strength. → His angle: "AI-native broadcast IT" consulting + tooling via Triseno.

## Post (Edit / Color / VFX / Captioning)
- **AI editing, color, rotoscoping, captioning** — `NOW`. Threat to assistant editors, captioners,
  roto artists. → His angle: AI-assisted post service or internal tool for studios.

## Production / Crewing / Scheduling / Ops (the underrated lane)
- **AI scheduling, crewing/staffing, call sheets, budgeting, script breakdown** — `NEAR`. Threat to
  coordinator/PM busywork. → His angle: **a production-ops agent for live events/broadcast** — the
  least crowded, most defensible Triseno play here, and it touches every department he already works with.

## Top disruption plays to pursue (ranked — keep current)
1. **AI live-clipping / social-cutdown service** — `NOW`, clear buyers (events, sports, broadcasters), uses his access.
2. **Production-ops agent** (scheduling/crewing/call sheets/budget) — `NEAR`, defensible, underserved, spans the whole stack.
3. **Managed gen-video shot service** — `NOW`, rides the strongest current wave (Kling/Luma broadcast-grade).

## Reality check to keep in view (strategic, not moral)
Live production is unionized (IATSE, etc.) and relationship-driven. The fastest wins are tools that make
existing crews faster (sell *into* the stack) before tools that remove roles wholesale — the second is
where the big disruption lives, but it moves through procurement, politics, and trust, not just tech.
Map both; sequence the easy adoption first to fund the bigger play.
