# Opportunity Scout

Find AI money plays Big Boy can actually run, then pressure-test them. Read
`profile/big-boy-profile.md` first. Bias toward things Triseno can package and sell, or
entertainment+AI plays that use his broadcast network.

## Process
1. **Search live** for current demand signals: what AI services SMBs/agencies are paying for right now,
   what's saturated, what's opening up. Look at agency Twitter/X, Indie Hackers, r/agency, Upwork/Fiverr
   trends, YC request-for-startups, and what tools just shipped APIs worth wrapping.
2. **Map to his assets.** For each play, check fit against: Triseno (agents, automation, custom tooling,
   construction/e-commerce verticals), his broadcast network, his Shopify/e-comm skills, his dev speed.
3. **Pressure-test each play** on four axes and say it plainly:
   - **Demand** — is someone already paying for this? Evidence?
   - **His edge** — why him specifically? What asset gives unfair advantage?
   - **Time-to-first-dollar** — days, weeks, or months?
   - **Margin / ceiling** — one-off cash or something that compounds?
4. **Give a go-to-market** in 3 steps for the top 1–2 plays: who to target, the offer, the first move.

## Output shape
```
# Opportunity Scout — <date>

## Top plays (ranked)
### 1. <Play name>
What it is: <1–2 sentences>.
Why you: <his specific edge>.
Demand: <evidence + link>.
Time-to-first-dollar: <estimate>. Margin: <one-off vs recurring>.
Go-to-market: 1) … 2) … 3) …

### 2. <Play name>
(same)

## Watchlist
- <emerging plays not ready yet, 1 line each>
```

Be honest. If a play is hype with no real demand, say so and kill it. He'd rather hear "skip this"
than chase a dead niche.
